- **Arrows** — highest sustained rate in the game. `:7411` (hunter: 2 geometries + 1 material) and `:12687` (goblin archer: 1 + 1). `timber` carries `userData.shared` so `disposeTree` will correctly leave it alone.
- **`rebuildHero()`** — `:5113-5117` `scene.remove(hero); hero = makeHero(); scene.add(hero);`. `makeHero` allocates every geometry and material fresh (~27 constructor calls, several in loops). Fires on every customiser swatch click (`:13457`) and on **every death** (`:19575`), which also drops the death pieces undisposed.
- **Peer nametags** — `:15563` overwrites `a.tag.material.map` with a fresh 512×128 `CanvasTexture` on any hp change and never disposes the old one. `netMyState` (`:15501`) sends `hp` unrounded and `updateFairyRest` heals at `dt * 0.02` every frame, so a wounded peer's hp differs on **every** 1/15 s wire tick and the texture is rebuilt pixel-identical 15 times a second for as long as they are healing. (The "3.8 MB/s from NaN" claim is refuted — JSON transport — but the healing-drip path is real and needs no hostile client.)
- **Peer avatars** — `:15534` (rebuild on a re-`hello`) and `:15543` (`netRemoveAvatar`) both only `scene.remove`. A whole hero group plus a `SpriteMaterial` and its map per join, leave, reconnect or look change; the rebuild path is exempt from the size cap, so repeated `hello`s under one id leak uncapped.
**Fix:** `disposeTree` after each remove; for the tag, dispose `material.map` and `material` by hand (`disposeTree` skips Sprite geometry but does not touch the map).

### B18. COSMETIC — Attack-tell rings are rebuilt for enemies removed earlier in the same frame and orphaned forever. *Found twice.*
`allEnemies()` memoises `slimes.concat(goblins, EXTRA, bosses…)` for a whole `updateTick` (`:12108`), and `:22873` `nearestEnemy(P.pos, 24)` seeds it before `updateExtraEnemies` and `updateGoblins` splice their corpses out. ### B20. COSMETIC — `nearestPlayerTo` returns prey it promises not to

# SUSPICIONS THAT SURVIVED

1. **`GEH.failed` latches the descent shut for the whole session.** `:15973` sets it in the build's `.catch`; `:15934` reads it in the dive gate; it appears in no teardown and no re-init (I read `:19135-19155` and `:17417-17423`) and `applySave` never touches it. One failed build and the objective at `:13422` is unreachable until a page reload. **What would settle it:** whether `gehBuildWorld` can actually throw on a valid seed. I found no path by reading. Cheap insurance regardless: clear `GEH.failed` in `gehTeardown` and on a successful build.
2. **The Goblin King's `"flee"` state has no `case`.** The sunrise flee at `:12889-12897` is handled above the switch and `continue`s out; the switch at `:12928-13008` has no `"flee"` arm, and `g.sink` only advances inside the guarded block. If `litNow` ever went false mid-sink the king would freeze permanently — the exact shape of the wraith `"chase"` bug. I could not construct a reachable path: `dayElev` rises monotonically at dawn and the only thing that rewinds `dayT` is sleeping, which kills every goblin first (see A1). **What would settle it:** whether any 3.0 beat — `dayBreak = 22`, `wakeTheWhirlpool`, or the shortening days — can drive `dayElev` back under `TUNE.goblins.dayElevation` inside the ~14 s a fleeing king survives.
3. **The open "player glitched from Gehenna to the overworld" bug is still unexplained.** The obvious candidate is **not** it: `lastSafeGeh = V3(0,0,0)` (`:15899`) is seeded by `gehDiveStep` at `:16021` while the body is still around y = −38, long before `geh` can be true, so `guardPlayerPosition` cannot teleport a recovered player to valley coordinates. Do not re-chase that one.
