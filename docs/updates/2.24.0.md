# 2.24.0 — The Still Kingdom

[Download the game](../../legend_of_peanits_v2.24.0.html) · [Full changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md)

Gehenna now unfolds in six chapters. A long road leads into a mostly empty golden kingdom, where the residents stand still and remember less than they should.

![The road through the Still Kingdom](2.24.0/kingdom.png)

At its center, a portal opens into an impossible white room. A flowing river, grassy banks, and trees fill its center; the edges remain white. Lucifer helps the children here, wearing a simple robe with the same restrained face style as the other characters.

![Lucifer's room and riverbank](2.24.0/lucifer-room.png)

Lucifer's face stays simple: black eyes, no nose, and a small smile. His robe has a draped collar and joined clasps.

![Lucifer character preview](2.24.0/lucifer.png)

When the hand arrives, Lucifer runs to stop it and grows to bear its weight. Your attacks make him shrink. His spoken pleas continue as the hand descends.

![Lucifer holding back the hand](2.24.0/lucifer-hand.png)

Wildmoor also gets a steadier camera, clearer quest guidance, improved enemy behavior, stronger fairy healing, quieter menus, and a repaired procession. [See the complete changes and checks](../../CHANGELOG.md#2240--the-still-kingdom).
